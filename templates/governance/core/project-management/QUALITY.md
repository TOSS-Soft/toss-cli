# Quality Governance

## Risk-Based Validation Outcomes

Validation depth is proportional to risk and must report PASS, FAIL, or UNKNOWN without substitution.

## Applicable Test Classes

Identify applicable unit, integration, end-to-end, security, compatibility, and manual validation classes.

## Exact Candidate

Evidence must identify the exact candidate, revision, environment, and inputs it covers.

## Evidence States

Evidence is CLAIMED, EVIDENCED, VERIFIED, FAILED, or UNKNOWN as supported by the record.

## Freshness

Evidence is valid only while it applies to the exact candidate and stated conditions.

## Conflicts

Conflicting evidence must be retained, investigated, and resolved or reported as UNKNOWN.

## Review Findings

Review findings require a recorded disposition and supporting evidence.

## Security and Secret Hygiene

Do not expose secrets; validate security controls appropriate to the work.

## Data Safety

Protect production data and require explicit authority for data mutation.

## Compatibility

Assess relevant interfaces, supported environments, migrations, and rollback effects.

## Documentation

Update operational and user-facing documentation when the change requires it.

## Definition of Done

Done requires accepted evidence for applicable criteria, known risks, and required documentation.

## Waivers

A waiver records a failed or unknown rule, scope, accepting authority, expiration, and compensating controls; it never changes the evidence state.
