# Work Management

## Objective Capture

Every material outcome starts with a verified Objective that preserves intent, outcome, scope, and acceptance conditions.

## Readiness

Work is ready only when authority, scope, dependencies, risks, and acceptance criteria are sufficiently known.

## Task States

Use DRAFT, READY, ASSIGNED, IN_PROGRESS, BLOCKED, DONE, DONE_WITH_WAIVER, or CANCELLED.

## Frozen Scope

An assigned Task scope is frozen until an approved change is recorded.

## Incidental Work

Discovered work outside frozen scope must be recorded and not silently performed.

## Change Control

Material changes require an updated record with authority, impact, and traceability.

## Agent Selection

Select an approved specialist by capability fit and least required authority.

## Assignment Boundary

Assignment requires a Task Contract that names authority, prohibited actions, evidence, workspace, and escalation conditions.

## Failure and Blocking

Failures and unknowns must be reported with available evidence; blocked work remains BLOCKED until resolved.

## Evidence Handoff

Specialists hand off technical evidence for PM evaluation; handoff does not close a Task by itself.

## Completion Authority

Only the PM may disposition completion evidence within delegated authority.

## State Checkpointing

Record material task state, decisions, risks, blockers, and next actions in canonical project state.
